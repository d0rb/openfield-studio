## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and ES modules served by the FastAPI app: no build step, no CDN, self-hosted fonts. Confirmed with the plan the owner approved on 2026-10-16.

## Users

Primary: a creator making short, shareable video clips alone on a laptop, usually late in the day in a dim room, reviewing footage and wanting a take they can post. Confirmed 2026-10-16.

Secondary: contest judges from Comfy, NVIDIA and LTX who open the app cold, with a "can't get it running" fallback, and decide within minutes. They succeed by acting like the creator.

## Product Purpose

OpenField Studio is an open-weights film studio built on the Comfy Developer Platform. A creator casts a character or product, directs a shot with a virtual camera rig, re-shoots a phone clip with a new character, and keeps every take with a verifiable receipt.

Success: a stranger sees real footage and a clear next action within seconds, gets a good take within a minute of connecting a backend (or sees recorded takes with no setup), and can trust the take was made with open weights only.

## Positioning

What the studio keeps and checks:

- Every take carries a checksummed receipt (prompts, seeds, workflow hash, exact model files and licences, open-weights audit, identity result) and can be remixed from the file.
- Cheap stills are approved before video compute is spent.
- Only open-weights models run, through the Comfy Developer Platform, on Comfy Cloud, the owner's own GPU box, or from recorded takes.

## Operating Context

Long sessions on a dim screen, reviewing footage: the interface stays colour-neutral around the footage: colour comes only from pictures, the takes and the interface art rendered on our own GPU. Renders take minutes, so waiting, progress, queue and failure states are core, not edge cases. Three backends: Comfy Cloud, a GPU box behind comfy-api-proxy (reached over an SSH tunnel), and Replay (recorded takes, no key and no GPU). Contest: Comfy's Open Source Higgsfield, deadline Fri Oct 30 2026, 09:00 PT.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Rooms, in the rail's order: the Lobby (the first screen since 2026-10-25: every app as a one-sheet poster that plays the recorded take proving it), then the stations Cast (reference sets with consent), Direct (camera rig, 49 moves in 7 groups (44 tested), approve stills, then shoot), Shots (one still, nine setups), Re-shoot (motion transfer onto a new character, or only the face from a Cast), Takes (the wall, ledger, receipts, remix, export; bridges, edits, dubs and extensions start from a take), and Chat (OpenCode with an open-weights model, Claude Code or Codex, through the studio's MCP tools).
- Models: LTX 2.5 (video and audio), Qwen-Image 2.1 (stills), Wan Animate 2 (motion transfer), Real-ESRGAN x4plus (upscale). No partner or API nodes.
- Twelve featured moves are promoted in the UI; the rest (thirty-seven, as of round 7) sit under "More moves". The interface prints no tested labels; the flags in the code say which moves held a real render: 44 of 49 (42 as of 2026-10-25, GPU session 9, when every bundled take's camera was also measured with optical flow; through object and the dolly zoom joined on 2026-10-26).
- The face check is local, report-only, with at most one automatic re-roll; faceless shots read "n/a".
- Replay never fakes a render: a recipe that was not recorded is refused with an explanation.
- Decided 2026-10-20 by the blind keyframing experiment: dolly in and crane up keep their anchor stills; orbit 90, arc left and track right render prompt-only (arc left went back to two stills on 2026-10-26, with the end still's facing anchored to the scene; see Round 13).

## Product Principles

1. Footage is the hero; the interface stays quiet around it.
2. Never fake a render: show recorded takes honestly, or say a backend is needed.
3. Proof over claims: every take shows its receipt and open-weights audit.
4. Cheap before expensive: approve stills before video is rendered.
5. Works first try for a stranger: no key, no GPU, one command.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable, AA contrast, state never carried by colour alone, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, readable at 1024 px wide and up (desktop first; a phone only needs to view takes).
